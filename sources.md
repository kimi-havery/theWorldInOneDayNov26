## 4. Britain — net migration falls to 77,000
- Office for National Statistics, "Long-term international
  migration, provisional: year ending June 2026", published
  26 Nov 2026
  https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/populationandmigration/internationalmigration
  Net migration 77,000 in the year ending June 2026, more than halved
  from 204,000 a year earlier; excluding the pandemic, the lowest
  since 1997. An estimated 738,000 people immigrated and 661,000
  emigrated. Non-EU net migration 258,000, down from 379,000.
- Reuters, "UK net migration falls to almost three-decade low,
  excluding pandemic slump", 26 Nov 2026
  https://dukecountry.fm/2026/11/26/uk-net-migration-falls-to-almost-three-decade-low-excluding-pandemic-period/
  Net migration has plunged about 90% since its peak of nearly a
  million (944,000, year to March 2023). Tighter immigration rules
  for workers, students and asylum seekers behind the fall.
